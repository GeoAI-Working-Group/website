# Geospatial Artificial Intelligence Working Group

A community-driven forum where researchers, practitioners and developers share methods, tools and lessons in applying AI to Earth observation and geospatial data.
</div>

## Pillars

<div class="pillars" markdown>
<div class="pillar" markdown>
### :material-school: Build Capacity
Hands-on exchanges, learning paths and training that grow applied ML and deep learning skills across the network. See [Capacity Building](capacity-building.md).
</div>
<div class="pillar" markdown>
### :material-earth: Applications
Co-developing fit-for-purpose Geo-AI workflows for real service needs, from crop mapping and forests to water, hazards and weather.
</div>
<div class="pillar" markdown>
### :material-share-variant: Knowledge Sharing
Open lectures, recorded talks, shared code, and the [EarthRISE Applied AI & Deep Learning Book](book.md).
</div>
</div>

## History

The working group grew out of a sustained, network-wide effort to build applied ML capacity, documented in the [book's introduction](https://nasa-earthrise.github.io/EarthRISE-Applied-Artificial-Intelligence-and-Deep-Learning-Book/).

<div class="timeline" markdown>
<div markdown>
<span class="when">2019 · Data Science and AI/ML Exchange</span> · Google Campus, Mountain View (Geo for Good)
Partners brought label data from their own service areas and began building ML workflows in Google Earth Engine. This laid the organizational foundation for the Geo-AI WG.
</div>
<div markdown>
<span class="when">2020 · TensorFlow Technical Exchange</span> · Virtual, 21–25 September
Five days of training, lectures and demos applying TensorFlow with Earth Engine, with each team working toward an ML workflow for a defined service application.
</div>
<div markdown>
<span class="when">2022 · TensorFlow Exchange at Geo for Good</span> · Google Bay View, October 4–6
Back in person; refined end-to-end ML workflows and formalized the Geo-AI Working Group.
</div>
<div markdown>
<span class="when">2022–2024 · AGU sessions</span> · Chicago, San Francisco, Washington D.C.
WG members convened and chaired AGU sessions each year on ML and Geo-AI for environmental challenges and sustainable development.
</div>
<div markdown>
<span class="when">2023 · Data Science and AI/ML Exchange</span> · Google Campus (Geo for Good)
Expanded to foundational models, LLMs and integrated Geo-AI workflows in a build-a-thon format.
</div>
<div markdown>
<span class="when">2023 · Earth Observation Foundational Model Hackathon</span> · Geo for Good, Dublin
Teams hosted and ran inference from Prithvi and SAM inside Earth Engine and Google Cloud workflows.
</div>
<div markdown>
<span class="when">2025 · Technical Work Planning: Geo-AI for Earth Science</span> · NSSTC, Huntsville, 3–7 February
Organizational leads formalized a shared strategic vision and roadmap for Geo-AI.
</div>
<div markdown>
<span class="when">2026 · EarthRISE Applied AI & Deep Learning Book</span> · First edition, June 2026
The Knowledge Sharing pillar in print: open, notebook-driven chapters from the community. [Read it →](book.md)
</div>
</div>

## Scope

Methods and tools that move geospatial AI from research into fit-for-purpose use, open by default: recorded talks, open materials and shared code.
