# About the Geo-AI Working Group

The Geospatial Artificial Intelligence Working Group (Geo-AI WG) is a community-driven forum where researchers, practitioners and developers share methods, tools and lessons in applying AI to Earth observation and geospatial data. Co-led by **Biplov Bhandari** and **Tim Mayer**.

## Pillars

<!-- DRAFT — confirm wording -->
1. **Foundation models** — multimodal and Earth-observation foundation models: use, evaluation, fine-tuning.
2. **Agentic Geo-AI** — LLM agents and tool-using workflows for geospatial science.
3. **Trust & evaluation** — benchmarks, validation, provenance, uncertainty.
4. **Capacity building** — the EarthRISE book, tutorials, the Developers Academy.
5. **Applications** — agriculture, forests, water, hazards, urban.

## Scope

Methods and tools that move geospatial AI from research to fit-for-purpose use. Open science by default: recorded talks, open materials, shared code.
