## Other ways in

- **Attend:** biweekly Wednesdays 10:00 CT on [Google Meet](https://meet.google.com/jmo-ojko-imx).
- **Subscribe:** [Google Group](https://groups.google.com/g/Geo-AI-working-group) · [Events RSS](feeds/events.xml)
- **Speak or suggest a speaker:** email the co-leads through the Google Group with a title and a short abstract.
- **Submit an event:** [open the event form](https://github.com/GeoAI-Working-Group/website/issues/new?template=submit-event.yml).
