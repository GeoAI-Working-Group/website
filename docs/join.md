# Get involved

- **Attend** — talks are open to all on [Google Meet](https://meet.google.com/jmo-ojko-imx). Add the [lecture calendar](feeds/lectures.ics).
- **Subscribe** — [Google Group](https://groups.google.com/g/Geo-AI-working-group) · [Events RSS](feeds/events.xml)
- **Speak or suggest a speaker** — email the co-leads via the Google Group with a title and short abstract.
- **Submit an event** — [open the event form](https://github.com/geoai-wg/website/issues/new?template=submit-event.yml).
