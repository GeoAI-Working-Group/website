# EarthRISE Applied AI & Deep Learning Book

Practitioner-focused applied AI and deep learning methodology and workflows leveraging Earth observations.
</div>

<a href="https://nasa-earthrise.github.io/EarthRISE-Applied-Artificial-Intelligence-and-Deep-Learning-Book/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><img class="book-cover-xl" src="../assets/img/book-cover.jpg" alt="Cover of The Applied Artificial Intelligence and Deep Learning Book"></a>

The *EarthRISE Applied Artificial Intelligence and Deep Learning Book* is the product of more than seven years of community-driven knowledge sharing within the Geo-AI Working Group. It puts the WG's **Knowledge Sharing** pillar into practice: a curated set of applied deep learning use cases, each pairing conceptual background with executable notebooks, written for geospatial practitioners who are new to deep learning.

Think of it as a "choose your own adventure": start at data preparation, or jump straight to the chapter that matches your domain.
